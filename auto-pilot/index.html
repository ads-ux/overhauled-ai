Common questions

Answered honestly.

What exactly is a "Digital Employee"?

It's an AI system trained specifically on your business that handles defined tasks automatically — answering enquiries, following up on leads, booking appointments, sending reminders. It's not a generic chatbot. It knows your business, your pricing, your services, and your tone. Think of it as a capable team member who works 24/7 and never misses a message.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It handles the repetitive, high-volume tasks that currently fall on you (or don't get done at all). Your staff — and you — focus on the work that actually requires a human. If anything, it makes your existing team more effective by removing the administrative noise.

What tools does it connect to?

It integrates with your existing tools — email inboxes, website contact forms, SMS, calendar apps (Google, Outlook, Calendly), and common CRMs. We assess your current setup during onboarding and build the integration accordingly. If you're not sure what you have, we'll figure it out together.

What if it says something wrong?

We build in guardrails so it only responds within what it's been trained on — it won't invent information or make promises you haven't approved. If it encounters a question it can't handle confidently, it escalates to you rather than guessing. And if something slips through, the 30-day support window exists precisely to catch and fix it.

How long does setup take?

Typically 5 to 10 business days from our initial call to go-live, depending on the complexity of your integrations. We handle everything. You'll review it before it goes live — nothing runs until you're satisfied with how it represents your business.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The $50/month subscription has no minimum term and no cancellation fee. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. The setup fee is one-time and non-refundable — but if you get to go-live and it's genuinely not what was described, talk to us. We'll make it right.
