What We Check

Every corner of your site — not just a surface scan

Mobile Speed Test

Most of your visitors are on a phone. We check how fast your site loads on mobile and flag anything that's making people leave before the page finishes.

Contact Form Test

We submit your contact form ourselves — so we know for certain whether leads are actually getting through. Broken forms are the #1 silent killer of small business websites.

Broken Link Check

Dead links frustrate visitors and hurt your Google ranking. We crawl every page and flag any link that leads nowhere — including links in your navigation and footer.

Local SEO Audit

Are you showing up when people in Guelph, Kitchener, or Waterloo search for what you do? We check your on-page signals, title tags, and local keyword targeting.

Google Business Profile Check

Your Google listing is often the first thing people see. We check that it's claimed, accurate, and pointing to the right place — and flag anything that's costing you trust.

Security & SSL Check

A browser warning that your site "isn't secure" will stop a visitor cold. We confirm your SSL certificate is valid, up to date, and not about to expire.

Core Web Vitals

Google uses page experience signals as a ranking factor. We check your Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift, and Interaction to Next Paint scores.

Content & Call-to-Action Review

Is it clear what you do, who you serve, and what a visitor should do next? We flag pages where the message is murky or the next step isn't obvious.

Questions

Answers to what you're probably wondering

What exactly do you fix — and what do you just report on?

We fix the top 3 highest-impact issues we find — things like a broken contact form, a missing SSL redirect, slow images, or a missing page title. For issues that need bigger changes (a site redesign, new content, a different platform), we document them clearly in the report with our recommendation. You'll always know exactly what was done and what still needs attention.

Do you need access to my website to do this?

For the audit itself, no — we can check most things from the front end. To make the fixes, yes, we'll need temporary login credentials or FTP access. We'll request only what we need, and you can revoke access once we're done. We treat your site with the same care we'd want for our own.

What if my site turns out to need a full rebuild?

We'll tell you honestly. If the audit shows that your current site has fundamental problems that can't be patched, we'll say so in the report — along with what a rebuild would realistically involve. If you decide to move forward with The Overhaul, your $297 Tune Up fee is credited toward the cost. You're not wasting money by starting here.

What platforms do you work with?

We work with WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify, Webflow, and most other common website platforms. If you're not sure what your site is built on, that's fine — just share the URL and we'll figure it out. The audit applies to any site. The fixes may vary slightly depending on platform access, which we'll clarify upfront.

Is the $297 credited if I upgrade to The Overhaul?

Yes — always, no expiry. If you complete a Tune Up and later decide you want a full site rebuild with The Overhaul, the $297 you paid comes straight off the project price. There's no time limit on that credit, and it doesn't require you to decide right away. Start here, take your time, and we'll be here when you're ready.

I'm in Guelph / Kitchener / Waterloo — do you work with local businesses?

Absolutely. We work with small businesses across Guelph, Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, and the surrounding area — and we specifically audit for local SEO signals like your Google Business Profile and local keyword presence. If ranking in your city matters to you (and it should), we pay extra attention to that as part of the Tune Up.
