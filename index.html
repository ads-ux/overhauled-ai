What's a Digital Employee and do I actually need one?
A Digital Employee does the job a person would normally do — 24 hours a day, without forgetting, without getting tired. More advanced: following up on quotes that went quiet, answering the 10 questions customers always ask before they buy, booking appointments. If leads are slipping through the cracks or you're spending time on repetitive back-and-forth, it pays for itself fast.
