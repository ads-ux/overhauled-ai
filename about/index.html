Why I built Overhauled.ai

I spent the last decade running Vrume — a private performance advertising network built on direct publisher relationships. We serve 2 billion+ ad impressions per month across 150+ countries. I know what makes someone click. I know what makes someone bounce. And I know exactly what a broken website looks like to someone who's spent years on the other side of it.

What I kept seeing was local businesses — plumbers, roofers, restaurants, clinics — spending money on ads, only to send people to a website that was loading slow on mobile, had a dead contact form, or was showing the wrong hours on Google. The ad worked. The website killed the sale.

I built Overhauled.ai to fix that. Not with a retainer. Not with a vague monthly SEO package. With specific, flat-rate fixes — find what's broken, fix it fast, tell you exactly what happened and why it matters. Then, if you want the whole system rebuilt properly, we do that too.

Everything is done remotely. I work with businesses across Guelph, Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, and beyond — location is never a barrier. I've built this to run from anywhere. That's the point.

The truth is, I love helping small businesses — and finding new ways to save money and generate more revenue is something I genuinely enjoy. That's what keeps this interesting.
